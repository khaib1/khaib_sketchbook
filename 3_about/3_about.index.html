About Me

My name is Khai Bui, and I'm a transfer student from Diablo Valley College, currently pursuing my degree in Visual Communications Design at San Francisco State University. I've always been interested in web design and the way visuals can communicate emotions and stories without needing many words. I like creating work that feels visually appealing but also has meaning and resonate with people. I'm taking this class because I want to continue growing as a designer and become more confident in expressing my ideas creatively. I hope to learn new techniques about CSS and HTML, experiment with different approaches, and better understand how to develop a fully functional website. Outside of design, I love traveling, exploring new places, and experiencing different cultures. I enjoy capturing everything from beautiful landscapes to unexpected moments along the way. Recording these moments is more than just taking pictures or videos, it's a way of preserving memories so I can look back on them.
